Starting

Pleni needs its key

Paste the key Pleni showed when it started. It is the long part after ?t= in its address. To see it again, run Pleni with --url on the computer that runs it. Your browser can save the key like a password.

Map

Reading your sessions…

    Who started whom Work going down Report coming up

    Drag empty space to move the map. Drag a tile onto another tile to say who it reports to; the Reports to menu in its side panel does the same. To zoom, hold Ctrl and scroll, pinch, or use the buttons. When the map is selected, the arrow keys move it, plus and minus zoom, and 0 fits it. The List view shows the same sessions as a table.
