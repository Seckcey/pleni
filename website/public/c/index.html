Find someone in Plenipo Community.

Someone shared their Community name with you, so you can find them in Plenipo.

  1. Download Plenipo, install it, and open it.
  2. Turn on Community in Settings → Switches, and sign in to your 8 West account (or make one).
  3. Open Community, choose Find someone, and type their Community name.

This page is the same for every name. It doesn't check whether a name is real, and it can't open anything in Plenipo.
